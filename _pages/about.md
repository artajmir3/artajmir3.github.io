I am a computer scientist working on machine learning, optimization, and optimal transport. I received my PhD in Computer Science from the University of British Columbia in May 2026, where I developed optimal-transport algorithms and metrics for 3D point-cloud alignment, with applications to cryogenic electron microscopy (cryo-EM) density maps, under the supervision of Anne Condon and Khanh Dao Duc. My research extends optimal-transport matching from one-to-one correspondence to partial and multiple-to-multiple matching, and I built OTAlignment, an open-source ChimeraX plugin that automates cryo-EM map alignment for structural biology researchers. I am currently looking for software engineer and research scientist roles.
